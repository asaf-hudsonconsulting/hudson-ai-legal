What we text, and to whom

We text only people who have personally asked us to arrange a showing for themselves. Every message is about that one person's own appointment. There are four kinds:

MessageWhen
Showing confirmation with date, time and street addressRight after the appointment is set
Up to three qualification questions the landlord requires: approximate credit score, combined annual income, and petsRight after the appointment is set
Reminder asking the person to reply CONFIRMAbout one hour before the showing
Cancellation notice if they did not confirmAbout 30 minutes before the showing

We send no marketing and no promotional content, we never text about anything other than the recipient's own appointment, and we never buy, rent or import phone number lists.

How people join, and how they leave

All three ways in are started by the person themselves:

  1. Our showing request form at this page, where a consent checkbox must be actively checked before the form can be sent.
  2. A rental listing inquiry the person submits on Zillow, StreetEasy, Apartments.com, Realtor.com or Trulia, entering their own phone number to ask about one of our listed apartments.
  3. Calling or texting our scheduling line at (201) 820-0700 to ask for a showing.

Message frequency varies, typically 2 to 5 messages per showing request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop all messages, or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of renting an apartment or of any purchase.
