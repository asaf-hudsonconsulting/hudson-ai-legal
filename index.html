What our text messages are for

We send text messages only to people who have asked us to arrange a showing for themselves. Every message is about that person's own appointment. We send a confirmation with the date, time and address, up to three qualification questions the landlord requires (approximate credit score, combined annual income, and pets), a reminder about one hour before the showing asking the person to reply CONFIRM, and a cancellation notice if they do not confirm.

We never send marketing or promotional messages, and we never message anyone on behalf of another business. We do not buy, rent, or use purchased phone number lists.

How people join

There are three ways someone comes to us, and all three are started by the person themselves:

  1. Our showing request form at this page, where a consent checkbox must be actively checked.
  2. A rental listing inquiry they submit on Zillow, StreetEasy, Apartments.com, Realtor.com or Trulia, which sends us their phone number and their request to be contacted about that apartment.
  3. Calling or texting our office at (201) 820-0700 to ask for a showing.

Message frequency, cost, and opting out

Message frequency varies, typically 2 to 5 messages per showing request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages, or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of renting an apartment or of any purchase.
