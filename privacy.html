What we collect. When you request an apartment showing we collect the information you give us: your name, phone number, email address, the apartment you are interested in, your preferred showing time, and answers to qualification questions (approximate credit score, combined annual income, guarantor availability, and pets). Calls with our scheduling assistant may be recorded and transcribed for quality and record keeping.

How we use it. We use this information only to schedule and confirm your showing, to determine whether the apartment's income requirement is met, to send you the text messages described in our SMS Terms and Conditions, and to give the showing agent (Team Soha, Compass) the details of your request.

Text messaging. If you provide your mobile number and request a showing, we may send you SMS/MMS messages about your showing (confirmations, qualification questions, reminders, and cancellations). Message frequency varies; message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help.

Sharing. We share your showing request details with the real estate agent conducting the showing (Team Soha at Compass) and with the service providers that operate our phone, calendar, and messaging systems (for example, Twilio, Retell, Cal.com, Make, and Google), solely to provide this service. We do not sell your personal information.

No sharing of mobile information for marketing. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the categories above exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Retention and security. We keep showing records for as long as needed to manage your request and comply with real estate and legal record-keeping obligations, and we protect them with reasonable administrative and technical safeguards.

Your choices. You can opt out of texts by replying STOP, ask us to stop calling by telling our assistant or texting "do not call", and request access to or deletion of your information by emailing asaf@hudsonconsulting.ai.
